About this page

A preview of the mini-sticks player: his kit, his body and all the clips in player.glb, in one page that opens from disk with no server and no network. It reads the model from player.glb.js and the kit's data from customizer_data.js, which must sit beside it.

The kit here is a preview. The page composes the kit's colour texture the way the kit tool does: from kit_mask.png, four colours and the glyph atlases, into the print rectangles of layout_kit.json. Nothing is saved to the files. A game composes its own kit at run time from the same files; IMPORT_NOTES.md, section 6, says how. The edges of the letters can differ slightly from the kit tool's, because the atlases are not the font.

Save player downloads player_settings.json: his number, name and colours, his height and body sliders (with the strength of every body shape by name), and the kit's pictures ready to use: the colour picture and, when he wears a logo, the jersey's bump and shine pictures without the crest's stitching. The file says inside what each part is.

Controls

  • Jersey: number, name, colours and a chest logo. Upload logo puts your picture (PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG) where the crest is, as large as fits; a see-through background stays see-through. Remove brings the crest back.
  • Body: height (5'5" to 6'7" for this model), his build, and a few presets (they set his face too). Face: jaw, chin, nose, cheeks, brow and ears, with the camera close on his face. The build and face sliders need a player.glb with body shapes; with one that has none, only the height changes him.
  • On the Body and Face tabs he stands at ease, so you can see all of him. Only this page has that pose: the game is played on the knees and player.glb has no standing clips. Pick a clip in the list to see it there; open the tab again to have him stand.
  • The page remembers what you set, the logo too (a small copy, at most 256 pixels), for the next time you open it.
  • Pick a clip in the list and it plays over and over at normal speed; a clip that is not a loop pauses for a moment on its last frame before it starts again.
  • Drag to turn round him, wheel or pinch to zoom, double-click (or double-tap) to put the camera back in front of him. ↑ ↓ pick the clip above or below; on a slider they move the slider.
  • He stays on the pedestal: a clip that travels plays in place, and a quarter turn still turns.
  • No ball is drawn: player.glb carries none. In the goalie ball clips and the glove save the hand is empty.
